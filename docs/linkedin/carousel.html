Why it matters

This is not only a statistic.

  • Peat. Kalimantan's peatlands store huge amounts of carbon. When they burn, they smoulder for weeks and release it.
  • Air. Smoke drifts over towns and across borders, and harms the health of those who breathe it.
  • Wildlife. Fire destroys the habitat of species such as the Bornean orangutan, already critically endangered.
Honest data

What these numbers can and cannot say.

  • A hotspot is not a fire. Satellites detect heat. One fire can produce several hotspots, and some are missed under cloud or smoke.
  • Hotspots are not burned area. Hectares come from a separate source (BNPB, reported by the press) at province level only.
  • Low-confidence detections are removed, then hotspots are matched to regency boundaries.
  • Nothing is invented. Where data is missing, the site says so.
