About the project

What is Visualize Karhutla?

  • An interactive hotspot map and a yearly summary for every regency in Kalimantan.
  • Built on NASA FIRMS satellite data, with burned-area figures from credible news sources.
  • "Karhutla Wrapped": a story you can share for your regency or all of Kalimantan.
  • Open source, and it grows through contributions from readers like you.

Before you start

What you need.

A free GitHub account

Submissions use GitHub Issues. Sign up for free at github.com/signup.

Everything is public

Anyone can read what you send. Please do not include personal data.

Reviewed first

A maintainer approves every submission before anything is published on the site.

The site interface is in formal Indonesian. This guide translates the labels you will see.

Articles and data

What makes a good submission?

Articles

  • From reputable media, government bodies, research institutes or environmental NGOs.
  • About forest and land fires in Indonesia.
  • A public link, no paywall if possible.
  • One or two sentences on why it matters.

Data

  • Burned area per regency or province (for example SiPongi, BPBD, official reports).
  • Always include the source link, the period and the unit.
  • Never invent numbers: leave blank if unknown.
  • Corrections: say what is wrong and give a reference.

For illustrators

Submit a template.

  • Any size or ratio: Instagram Story 9:16, desktop landscape, or anything else.
  • PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF. Up to 12 MB each, up to 6 files. No SVG.
  • Give your name for credit and, optionally, a portfolio link.
  • Tick both confirmations about ownership and free use.

After you submit

What happens next.

1

You submit

Your form becomes a public GitHub issue. Nothing is published yet.

2

Maintainer reviews

Checks the content, credit and that the work is yours to share.

3

Approved

The maintainer adds the label setujui-templat.

4

Automatic publish

An automated workflow optimizes the image, saves it to the repository and redeploys the site.

5

It goes live

Your template appears under "Community templates" with your name. The issue is closed with a thank-you.

Only approved work enters the repository. Declined or unclear submissions stay as issues and may get follow-up questions.

For developers

Contribute code.

  1. Fork the repository and create a branch.
  2. Run npm install, then npm run dev.
  3. Make your change and run npm run build until it passes.
  4. Open a pull request describing what and why.

House rules

  • UI text is formal Indonesian ("Anda", not "kamu").
  • Every number shows its source and date.
  • Never invent data; leave it empty if unavailable.
  • Details: CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository.
