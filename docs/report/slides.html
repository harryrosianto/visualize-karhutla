Quality

Smoother, sturdier, easier to share.

  • Mobile-first redesign. Poster-style hero, readable on small screens.
  • Share images show the final state, not a mid-animation frame.
  • Link previews for every Wrapped page, so links look right in chats.
  • Resilient updates. The site recovers by itself after a new release.
  • Accessible by default. Keyboard control and reduced-motion support.
  • Open source. Contribution guide, issue forms and a how-to deck.

What to improve

There is still a lot to do.

1

Daily automatic data refresh so the map never goes stale.

2

Burned area per regency (today: province level, from news reports).

3

More years of history for context beyond 2025.

4

A faster map on slow phones (lighter hotspot data).

5

Automated tests for pages and share images, and an English version.

We need your contribution.

Data: burned-area figures, local reports, corrections. Assets: illustrations, photos, designs. Also: articles, code, translation.
