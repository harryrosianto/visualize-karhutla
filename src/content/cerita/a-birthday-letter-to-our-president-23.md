Dear Mr. President,

Happy birthday. Today you turn seventy-five, and somewhere in the palace a cake is surely waiting for you. But look closer, sir. Your cake this year is an island. Its frosting is peat, its layers are forest, and its land is on fire. The smoke rising from it does not come from the candles. So please, blow them out. Blow them out now, before there is nothing left to celebrate.

So hear us clearly, Mr. President. We do not want another press conference, another helicopter photo, another promise that disappears when the rain comes. We want you to name the burners, punish them, and stop letting them come back. Because no palace wall is high enough to keep out the smoke, and no title is grand enough to escape the judgment of history.

We breathe the same air. We stand on the same burning island.

If we burn, you burn with us.
